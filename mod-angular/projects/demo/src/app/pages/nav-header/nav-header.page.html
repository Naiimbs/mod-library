Nav Header

The UI-shell navigation header that guides users through a site. It appears at the top of every page and holds the platform logo, primary menu items (with optional sub-menu dropdowns), and action buttons. Adapts across desktop (>960), tablet (600–960), and mobile (<600) breakpoints, supports contained or full-width layouts, and mirrors fully for RTL. Modeled on Figma frames 30150:148751 (Nav Header), 148312 (Menu Item), 148445 (Action), 148860 (Menu), 148877 (Sub-menu), 148183 (Sub-menu Item), 148742 (Item Icon).
