Menu

A floating list of actions or options, opened from a trigger (button, icon, or nav item) or used as a context menu. Items can group under labels, carry a leading icon, and show a trailing element (count text, icon, tag, or switch). Six item states — default, hover, pressed, selected, focused, disabled — plus full RTL. Modeled on Figma frames 30195:22214 (Menu) and 30195:21865 (Menu list item).
