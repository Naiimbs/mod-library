Building Permit

Ministry Life experience Related platform

Service description example: to buy a plot to build your house, this requires documenting the sale and purchase process in the notarial offices or notary services to register the property in your name.

Service Level Agreement
  1. Login to the National Single Sign-on portal via the Balady platform.
  2. Select Construction License Services, then Issue Construction.
  3. License Services, then the required municipality.
  4. Select the engineering office, then enter the number and year of issuance of the building survey, and attach a copy of the deed.
  5. Submit the request to the identified engineering office, then complete the information and attachments in the engineering office and send it to the municipality.
  6. Verify the information of the request, attachments and approvals by the municipality.
  7. Approve the request and issue a SADAD payment. Pay the fees and print the license.
  • The applicant must be the owner of the land or an authorised representative.
  • The land deed must be electronic and up to date.
  • A licensed engineering office must supervise the construction drawings.
  • All municipal fees and violations must be settled before submission.
  • A copy of the electronic land deed.
  • An approved building survey report.
  • Engineering drawings approved by a licensed office.
  • National ID of the applicant or the authorised representative.
