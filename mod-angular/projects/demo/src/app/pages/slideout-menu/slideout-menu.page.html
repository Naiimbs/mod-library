Slideout Menu

A panel that slides in from the side of the screen, triggered by a button. It surfaces navigation, options, or extra content without cluttering the main view. Composed of a header (featured icon + title + description + close), a scrollable body (a menu-items list or a custom content slot), and an actions footer. Anchors left or right, ships gray or white, and mirrors fully for RTL. Modeled on Figma frames 23360:17219 (Slideout Menu) and 23521:31454 (Slideout Menu Header); the body reuses the Menu component.
