Digital Stamp

A trust indicator that confirms users are interacting with a verified and secure Saudi government source. The component renders as a thin notification bar across the top of a page; tapping "How you know?" expands it to show verification cards (URL extension + HTTPS) and the DGA registration record. Modeled on Figma frames 17504:520 (main) and 25079:5361 (extension variants).

Live demo

Digital Stamp

The stamp ships in two device layouts (desktop bar / mobile bar) and six URL-extension variants. Toggle Opened to reveal the verification cards and the DGA registration record.

Click the stamp's How you know? button (or the Opened toggle) to switch between the collapsed and expanded views. The Extension select swaps the URL-extension verification card content per Figma frame 25079:5361.

Props / Configuration

All available CSS classes and the high-level slots that compose a stamp.

Class Type Description
.digital-stampRequiredBase container. Holds the collapsed bar and (when opened) the verification cards + registration card.
.digital-stamp--openedStateExpands the stamp to show the extension and registration sections.
.digital-stamp--mobileModifierSwitches the bar to the compact mobile layout where the toggle button drops below the flag + text row.
.digital-stamp--rtlModifierRight-to-left layout. Equivalent to setting dir="rtl" on the element.
.digital-stamp__contentSlotThe collapsed-state bar: .digital-stamp__flag, .digital-stamp__text, and .digital-stamp__toggle.
.digital-stamp__flagSlotSmall Saudi flag indicator at the leading edge of the bar.
.digital-stamp__textSlotThe short attestation sentence.
.digital-stamp__toggleSlotThe "How you know?" link button with chevron. Rotates 180° when opened via the existing .digital-stamp__toggle-icon rule.
.digital-stamp__extensionSlotContainer for the two verification cards. Only visible when .digital-stamp--opened is present.
.digital-stamp__verification-cardSlotA single icon + title + description verification card (used twice in the extension section).
.digital-stamp__registrationSlotThe DGA registration row at the bottom of the opened stamp.
data-card-expandableJS hookNot required — stamps register automatically via initDigitalStamps() on the .digital-stamp selector.

Anatomy

The collapsed bar has three parts: the Saudi flag indicator, the attestation text, and the "How you know?" toggle. Tapping the toggle reveals two verification cards (URL extension and HTTPS) and the DGA registration record.

Mobile Layout

The compact mobile bar drops the "How you know?" toggle below the flag + text row to fit narrow viewports. When opened, the verification cards stack vertically instead of sitting side-by-side. Triggered by .digital-stamp--mobile.

RTL Support

Arabic and other right-to-left layouts mirror the bar so the flag sits at the trailing edge, the "How you know?" link reads from the right, and the verification cards reverse direction. Triggered by .digital-stamp--rtl or dir="rtl".
